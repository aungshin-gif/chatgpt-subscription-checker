Frequently asked questions

What does Star Checking check?

ChatGPT — your plan (Plus / Pro / Free), expiry date and auto-renewal. Grok — your plan (SuperGrok / Heavy / Free) and this week's usage.

Is my Session stored anywhere?

No. Your Session is only used for the check and cleared right after. It is never saved or shown to anyone.

What does a 401 error mean?

Your Session has expired. Get a fresh Session and check again.

What does a 403 error mean? (Grok)

The Grok login has expired. Run 'grok login' again on the server and check again.

Guidelines

  • Never share your Session with anyone — it is like your password.
  • Check only your own accounts.
  • Tokens expire. If a check asks you to re-login, do it and try again.
  • Never paste your Session in chats or screenshots.
